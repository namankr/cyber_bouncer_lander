The New Shift: Autonomous AI Agents

Traditionally, hackers used static, predictable scripts to attack websites. Today, they deploy smart **AI agents** that act like independent human operators.

These bots mimic human habits—adding random delays between clicks and pretending to use normal browsers—while systematically scanning your system for flaws at machine speed.

Recent Real-World Attack: Automated scanning networks recently targeted vulnerable PHP systems globally, compromising targets instantly before traditional security tools even registered the activity.

Why Traditional Walls Fail

If you tighten your security filters to stop these human-mimicking bots, you end up accidentally blocking real human customers. Keep them loose, and the automated AI slips right past.

The Cyber Bouncer Approach

Instead of just looking for known malicious text patterns, Cyber Bouncer acts like an observant guard at the door. It studies traffic rhythms, behavior anomalies, and underlying connection handshakes to instantly separate real human customers from spoofed bots.
